How to use Seclusa Weather

  1. Search or locate — type a city in the search box, or tap the crosshair button to use your current location.
  2. View the forecast — the main card shows today's conditions, sun/moon times, and an arc of daylight. Below it are the hourly, 7 or 14 day forecast, and a wind compass for your area.
  3. Switch units — tap the units button (°C) in the top bar to change temperature, wind speed, and visibility units.
  4. Explore conditions — the Conditions card groups precipitation, wind, humidity, UV index, visibility, pressure, air quality, and pollen.
  5. Works offline — the last forecast is saved, so the app works without a connection.
  6. Install it — tap the install banner on Android, or on iPhone use Share → "Add to Home Screen".

Deletes the saved city, coordinates, settings, and every cached forecast from this device.
